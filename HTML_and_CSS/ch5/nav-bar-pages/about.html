Our story

About the passionate Japanese kitchen in Tallinn

Welcome to Yoko's kitchen! My journey began in Kyoto, where I learned about the traditional art of Washoku - cooking that respects the purity of ingredients and the changing seasons.

I have now set up a cozy studio under the pines of Nõmme, where I combine authentic Japanese flavors and the best local ingredients. My goal is to break the myth that Japanese food is limited to sushi.

Yoko's Kitchen is a place for those who appreciate healthy, balanced and visually beautiful food. Whether it's an exciting cooking class or a memorable catering service - I bring a piece of Japan directly to your plate.

What makes us special

Authenticity: We use traditional techniques and seasonings imported from Japan.

Personal atmosphere: Our courses are small and intimate, ensuring attention to each participant.
