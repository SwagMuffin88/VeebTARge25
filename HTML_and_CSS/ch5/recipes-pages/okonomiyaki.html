Okonomiyaki

Japanese savoury pancakes

"Okonomiyaki" translates to "grill as you like". It is a cabbage-based pancake where you can add seafood, meat, vegetables etc.

How to prepare:

Mix together flour, eggs and shredded cabbage. Fry on both sides until golden brown and serve with a special sauce and mayonnaise.
