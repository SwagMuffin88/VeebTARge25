Mizutaki (Chicken hot pot)

A warming and healthy one-pot meal

Mizutaki originates from Fukuoka and is Japan's nabemono, or king of casseroles. It is prepared right on the table in a pot where chicken and vegetables simmer in a mild broth.

How to prepare:

Cut chicken, Chinese cabbage, shiitake mushrooms and tofu into bite-sized pieces. Cook in lightly seasoned kombu broth. The magic of Mizutaki lies in its simplicity - the ingredients retain their pure flavor.

How to serve: The pieces are taken out of the pot and dipped in a refreshing ponzu sauce.
