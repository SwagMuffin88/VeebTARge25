Yakitori (Grilled chicken)

Japanese street food classic

Yakitori is a popular Japanese snack consisting of pieces of chicken grilled on bamboo skewers and covered in a sweet tare sauce.

Ingredients:

  • 500g chicken thigh meat
  • Leek
  • Soy sauce, mirin, sugar (for the sauce)
