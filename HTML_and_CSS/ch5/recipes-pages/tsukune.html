Tsukune (Chicken meatballs)

Juicy chicken meatballs with a sweet glaze

Tsukune is a classic Japanese izakaya, or pub food. They are balls of minced chicken with a slightly smoky flavor, usually grilled and coated with a tare glaze made from thick soy sauce.

Ingredients:

  • 500g chicken mince
  • 1 tbsp grated ginger
  • 2 tbsp chopped onion or leek
  • 1 tbsp starch and a little salt

Form the mixture into small balls, thread them onto bamboo skewers and grill over medium heat until golden brown. Brush with sweet soy sauce several times.
